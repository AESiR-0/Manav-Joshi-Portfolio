Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This covers the information you give when you apply for, pay for or attend Sunday Morning Build Club or a Chemistry Labs Practical. It is held by Manav Joshi, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

What we collect

  • What you type into the application form: name, phone number, email, the city you live in, gender, age group, links, what you're building, why you want to come, and how you heard about us.
  • Our WhatsApp conversation with you.
  • Whether you paid, came, and what you wrote on the feedback form afterwards.

What we use it for

  • Deciding who to invite, so each room has the right mix of people.
  • Sending your invite, payment details, reminders and the venue on WhatsApp.
  • Improving the events, using your feedback.

Who else sees it

Our small team running the event, and the services that store it or carry our messages: Google (forms and sheets), Cloudflare (our database), WhatsApp, and our payment provider. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Payments

Card and bank details go directly to the payment provider. We never see or store them.

Deleting your information

Write to work@manavjoshi.com and we will delete it. We keep a record of a payment only as long as the law requires.
