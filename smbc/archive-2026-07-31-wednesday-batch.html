What happens in the room ↓

Why this exists

Your own work is the only thing with no deadline.

So it moves. Every week. Client work has a date on it. The job has a date on it. Yours waits for a free morning that never arrives.

This is that morning.

The morning

Three hours, and none of it is a talk.

✱ One rule: no mundane work.

No client deliverables, no inbox, no admin. You have six days for the things that pay. This morning is only for the ambitious thing.

What you leave with

Nobody demos anything.

Work built to be shown off in three hours gets abandoned by Wednesday.

You take home the project you'd been putting off for months, finally started, and eight people who know what you said you'd do.

We don't collect proof at 12:40. We ask the following Sunday what happened to the thing you started.

Who's in the room

Eight people who do eight different things.

A room full of people from your own industry gives you depth. Depth is already on the internet.

Someone who has never built software asking why your signup takes four steps. That you can't get anywhere else.

How you get in

Two things get you a seat.

You know what you're working on next.

A three-month goal or a ten-year one. Either is fine. Vague is not.

You bring something the room can use.

Your craft, your contacts, or an honest opinion on someone else's plan.

You don't need a company, funding or a track record. Some people here have shipped a lot. Some haven't shipped anything yet. The person who finally moves after eight months of stalling is the most useful person in the room.

Before you ask

Questions

I haven't really built anything yet. Should I still apply?

Yes. The bar is knowing what you're working on next, not what you've finished. If you're stuck at the start, this is the room for it.

Is this a networking event?

No. Nobody pitches, nobody hands out cards. You'll leave with people's numbers because you spent three hours inside each other's actual problems.

When do I pay?

Only if you're invited. Wednesday's message tells you how. Nothing is collected on this page.

What do I bring, and where is it?

Whatever you build with, and one project you've been avoiding. It's in Vastrapur, Ahmedabad. The exact address comes with the invite.

I asked and didn't get in. What now?

Your request stays with us and you don't apply again. Eight invites go out every Sunday, and you're already in line for the next one.
