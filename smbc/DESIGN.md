# SMBC page — design brief (2026-07-31 rebuild)

## Phase 0 brief

1. **Product + audience.** The front door of an invite-only weekly builders' club
   in Ahmedabad. For ambitious people whose own work never gets their best hours.
2. **Emotional tone — two adjectives: BRIGHT, CERTAIN.**
   Bright carries Manav's "energetic, positive". Certain carries "ambitious,
   audacious" — audacity is only credible when it's stated calmly. Nothing on this
   page begs, counts down, or shouts.
3. **Aesthetic direction: editorial poster / zine.** Big rounded display type on
   cream paper, mono facts, one photograph used four ways. Committed, not hedged.
4. **Fonts — three, each with exactly one job.**
   - Fredoka → the club talking. Headlines, the name, buttons.
   - Fraunces → the truth said quietly. The two statement lines only.
   - Space Mono → facts, labels, clock, form. Never a headline.
5. **Signature element: the CRT.** The hero photograph is a real TV broadcasting
   the club's name. That screen becomes the page's recurring device — the morning's
   clock appears inside a scanlined, mint-glowing broadcast panel. Secondary motif:
   the `>>` chain, which is Manav's own approved copy device.

## Who lands here, and what the page owes each

| Arrival | State | What the page does |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (Manav DM'd them) | Half-committed, wants the form | Hero CTA jumps straight to the form; sticky bar follows them down |
| **Curious cold** (saw a story) | No idea what happens | Section 3 is a real clock, in concrete times |
| **Skeptical cold** | Assumes networking / paid coworking | Killed in section 3 ("none of it is a talk", "no mundane work") and FAQ 2 |

Dominant traffic is an Instagram story tap: mobile, portrait, one thumb, feed-brain,
about four seconds of patience. So the page is **image-led and mobile-first**, and the
first screen answers what / when / where / am-I-in without a paragraph.

## Emotional sequence (the negotiation order)

Recognised → certain → slightly held to a standard. Never flattered, never chased.

1. **Hero** — the photograph. Recognition line, the date, one CTA.
2. **The mirror** — why your own work is always last. Enemy is misallocation.
3. **The morning** — the real clock. Kills uncertainty, kills the seminar fear.
4. **What you leave with** — movement, not artifacts. No demo, deliberately.
5. **The room** — eight different people. Kills "I'll be the least impressive one."
6. **The bar** — the standard stated plainly, then the disqualification undone.
7. **The ask** — facts strip, then four fields.
8. **Questions** — five, all of them real DM questions.
9. **Footer** — the door stays open, one joke in the corner.

## Fears this page is built to disarm

1. *Will I be the least impressive person there?* → the bar is direction, not track
   record (section 6 + FAQ 1). Stated as a standard, never as reassurance.
2. *Is this a networking event?* → section 3 and FAQ 2.
3. *What do I actually DO for three hours?* → the clock, to the minute.
4. *Do I pay before I know I'm in?* → checkbox says "if I'm invited"; FAQ 3.
5. *I'll apply and hear nothing.* → "Wednesday, everyone hears back, either way",
   stated twice, before and after submitting.
6. *10 am Sunday is my only sleep-in.* → footer joke owns it instead of hiding it.

## Rulings honoured (do not relitigate — see vault `pairs/`)

- Never justify the price. ₹449 appears twice as a plain fact, never explained.
- Scarcity is structural: "eight invites go out every Sunday". Never a countdown.
- State the reader's action, not our process.
- Process copy is a chain (`>>`), never prose.
- "Room" is a space, never a schedulable unit.
- No demo claim anywhere. Copy claims *stuck things moved*, never *things got built*.
- Gratitude wall is not on the page. It's discovered in the room.
- Address arrives with the invite.
- FAQ answers only what someone would type into a DM before applying.

## Image strategy

One photograph, four crops. No stock, no AI image — a generated image beside a real
one breaks the world, and this photo already *is* the tone.

- **Hero** — full poster. Carries the club's name so no headline has to.
- **The morning** — tight crop on the CRT, behind the broadcast panel.
- **The room** — crop on the legs and sneakers. Kills "corporate meetup" in one look.
- **Footer** — the sparkle corner, faded.

Desktop is a split hero (type left, photo right in a column that matches the photo's
0.558 aspect) rather than a cover-crop, because cover-cropping a portrait poster into
a 16:9 viewport destroys it.

## Blacklist audit

No purple/blue gradient, no gradient text, no emoji section icons, no three equal
cards, no single-column-everything, no default font, no lorem, no placeholder images,
no white background with floating shadow cards. Section rhythm deliberately varies:
full-bleed hero → narrow centred statement → wide clock panel → offset room block →
narrow form → list.
