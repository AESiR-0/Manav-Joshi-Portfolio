Before you ask

Questions

How does the invite actually work?
You send the form. Within 24 hours you get a yes or a no on WhatsApp. A yes comes with a private link that holds your seat for 24 hours: you pay the ₹449 there, and the address arrives. Let the 24 hours pass and the seat goes to the next person.
When do I pay, and what if something comes up?
Only after you're invited, never on this page. If something comes up before Sunday, say so and the seat rolls to a later one. If you just don't turn up, it doesn't.
I asked and didn't get in. What now?
Your request stays with us and you don't apply again. Eight seats open every Sunday and you're already in line for the next one. If it's a no three times over, you'll hear that plainly instead of being left waiting.
What do I bring, and where is it?
Whatever you build with, and one project you've been avoiding. It's in Vastrapur, Ahmedabad. The exact address comes with the invite.
Is there a group I can join without applying?
No. The WhatsApp group is only for people who have been on a Sunday. You get the link after your first one.
Do I have to finish something in three hours?
No, and nobody demos anything. Work built to be shown off in three hours gets abandoned by Wednesday. We ask the following Sunday what happened to the thing you started.
