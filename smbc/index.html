What happens

We'll work on your next ambitious project.

You say what you're working on and what's in the way. The room cuts it down with you until it's small enough to actually start. They offer what they have. You say what you need.

Then the room goes quiet and you start it.

One rule: no mundane work.

I am not asking you to do your mundane work on Sunday morning. I am asking you to work on your most ambitious project. Something you don't get a chance to do in the chaos of the week.

My project isn't that ambitious. Should I still apply? +

The bar is that you're actually building it. Not how big it looks.

I'm not building an app. Does this count? +

Builders are not just coders. Writers, filmmakers, founders, students. If you're making something, this is for you.

What actually happens? +

You say your goal and your challenges. The room cuts both down until they're specific, offers help, and hears your asks. The last 45 minutes are focused work. Nobody's swapping business cards.

What do I bring? +

An empty mind and your favourite diary. We'll figure the rest.

How long, and where? +

10 AM to 1 PM. The city changes from one Sunday to the next, so every date in the form says where that one is. You get the address with your invite.

I applied. Then what? +

We read every one. A few get an invite that week. If it isn't you, your request doesn't die, it carries to the next Sunday and sits above the new ones. You never have to apply twice.
