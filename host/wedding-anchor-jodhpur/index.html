Questions people ask

Do you travel to Jodhpur?

Yes, from Ahmedabad, and I stay for the full run of a multi-day wedding rather than flying in per function.

Which languages do you host in?

Hindi, Gujarati and English, all three at native comfort, switched live to match the room.

What makes a fort or palace wedding different to host?

The crowd spreads out and the venue competes with you. You win those rooms by pulling people in tight for the moments that matter instead of trying to shout across the courtyard.
