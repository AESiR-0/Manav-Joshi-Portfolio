Languages

Gujarati and Hindi carry most of a Surat room, with English for the corporate segments. Surat is mixed enough — Saurashtrian, Marwari, Bhojpuri, South Gujarat — that a host who only speaks one of those loses half the floor.

Travelling to Surat

A few hours up the highway from Ahmedabad, or the train. Day events I travel for the same day; multi-function weddings I stay over. Travel and stay are billed at cost, never marked up.
